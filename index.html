The Sourcing Workflow

From wishlist to driveway in four steps.

Every vehicle follows the same auditable, four-step process — the same process detailed in full on our How It Works page.

1

Wishlist & Pre-Approval

Tell us your budget, must-have features, and mileage ceiling. We confirm cash-in-hand or Tier-1 bank financing pre-approval before any bidding begins.

2

Live Auction Matching

Our registered dealer principals match live OPENLANE and Manheim Milton lots to your wishlist and run a pre-bid diagnostic audit before placing any bid.

3

Flatbed Haul & DriveON Certification

The won vehicle is flatbed-hauled to our Etobicoke hub for a PSI inspection and full DriveON Safety Standards Certificate before it ever reaches you.

4

Open-Book Invoicing & Delivery

You receive the raw auction invoice alongside every pass-through cost, then turnkey flatbed delivery straight to your driveway anywhere in the GTA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers, before you ever pick up the phone.

Wholesale proxy sourcing allows retail and business buyers to purchase vehicles directly from closed dealer auctions like OPENLANE and Manheim Milton through a licensed OMVIC dealer. The buyer pays the exact auction hammer price, pass-through transport, PSI inspection, and DriveON safety costs, plus a transparent $1,500 flat sourcing fee — saving $3,000 to $6,000 compared to traditional retail dealership markups.
Yes. GTA Wholesale Auto Sourcing connects directly to Tier-1 Canadian lenders — such as TD, Scotiabank, and RBC — through Dealertrack and RouteOne. Customers receive true prime bank approval rates without dealer interest rate markups.
Yes. Every sourced vehicle includes a mandatory wholesale Post-Sale Inspection (PSI) guarantee protecting against major powertrain and structural frame defects, as well as a comprehensive Ontario DriveON Safety Standard Certificate before registration.
The $1,500 CAD flat sourcing fee covers live auction bid representation at OPENLANE and Manheim Milton, a pre-bid diagnostic audit, and open-book invoicing with zero markup on the hammer price. It does not include the vehicle price itself, transport, PSI inspection, or DriveON safety certification — those are billed at direct pass-through cost.
Yes. GTA Wholesale Auto Sourcing operates under Vector Auto Sourcing Inc., a General Dealer (Used) registered with the Ontario Motor Vehicle Industry Council (OMVIC) — the legal requirement to bid at closed dealer-only auctions such as OPENLANE and Manheim Milton on a client's behalf.
Most vehicles are matched and won at auction within 5 to 10 business days of an approved wishlist, followed by 3 to 5 business days for flatbed transport, PSI inspection, and DriveON safety certification at the Etobicoke hub before driveway delivery.
